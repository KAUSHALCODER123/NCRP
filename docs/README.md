## The short version

**Deadline: 28 Aug 2026, 20:00 IST.** Solo entry. Submitting 28 Aug at 14:00.

Sahaay argues two things:

1. **Freeze first, ask later** — the golden hour belongs to intercepting money, not to filling a form.
2. **Freeze the amount, not the person** — one report should not paralyse an innocent merchant's entire account for months.

Three numbers carry the pitch: **2.18%** of reported stolen money is actually returned to victims · **1.4%** FIR conversion in 2025 · **28.15 lakh** complaints that year.
